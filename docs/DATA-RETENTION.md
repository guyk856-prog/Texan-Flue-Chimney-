# Customer export, archive and deletion procedure

[REVIEW WITH COUNSEL BEFORE USE]

The owner must adopt and verify the policy before customer use. The electronic-signing disclosure states seven years after issue, with longer retention for a hold or unresolved matter. This is an operating policy for review, not a claim about an automatically determined legal obligation.

1. Verify the requester’s authority using the company’s existing contact process. Do not treat possession of a share link or an IP address as identity proof. Record a case reference and the request/date.
2. ADMIN opens the customer and chooses **Download customer records ZIP**. The export includes scoped customer/property/job records, proposals/invoices/payments/receipts, inspections, signing/consent snapshots and attached evidence. Review it before delivering it securely. The export action is recorded in the audit log.
3. Choose **Archive for a privacy request**, enter the case reference and reason. This hides active service access, revokes associated share links and records the action. It does not pretend that archiving is physical erasure or destroy issued financial/signing evidence.
4. The owner assesses the seven-year period, open matters and any hold with counsel. Keep a hold reference in the customer’s administrative records. Explain any retained records and the reason to the requester. Do not promise immediate removal where retention still applies.
5. Physical erasure after the approved period is an operator-controlled maintenance action, not an application Delete button. First export and take/verify an encrypted backup. Identify every subject record, immutable document version, private original/display file and relevant audit payload; check that any shared file is not needed by another record. In a controlled maintenance window, the database operator must remove the eligible records/files together and record the case outcome separately. Database immutability guards deliberately prevent casual deletion; do not disable them from a web route or silently change a signed snapshot.
6. Backups expire on a separate rolling window of at least 30 days. A restored database must reapply archived/deletion decisions before customer access resumes. Do not immediately destroy the last verified recovery copy in response to an unverified request.

The application implements export, revocation and archive. It does not implement an automated seven-year purge or counsel’s retention/hold decision. Physical erasure remains a documented operator procedure requiring case review; no placeholder is labelled as an erasure implementation.
